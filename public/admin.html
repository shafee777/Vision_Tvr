Frame Measurement Guide (All measurements in millimeters — mm)
Lens width (lens_width_mm): Width of one lens horizontally (typically 44–60 mm).
Bridge width (bridge_width_mm): Distance across the nose bridge between lenses (typically 14–24 mm).
Temple length (temple_length_mm): Side arm length from hinge to ear tip (typically 135–150 mm).
Frame height (frame_height_mm): Vertical height of the lens/frame (recommended, mm).
Total frame width (total_width_mm): Overall front width from left hinge to right hinge (recommended, mm).
Tip: Look inside any eyeglass temple arm — e.g. 52 □ 18 - 145 means Lens 52 mm, Bridge 18 mm, Temple 145 mm.
